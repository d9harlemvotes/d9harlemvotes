Our impact extends beyond the ballot box. D9 Harlem Votes® partners with community-based organizations, city agencies, and state agencies to make information and resources accessible to Harlem residents.

IMPACT TO DATE

Measuring our impact.

D9 Harlem Votes® Boots on the Ground is our signature voter-registration activation centering conversations about voting and voter rights. Since 2024, D9 Harlem Votes® has been on the ground serving Harlem. Our impact is measured through our tracked engagement with every person we meet. We provide civic and voter education to ineligible, potential, eligible, undecided, and registered voters. We assist in navigating the registration process. Our goal is to keep Harlem informed, prepared, and ready to exercise the power of their vote.
