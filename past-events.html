D9 Harlem Votes® Community Conversations

Setting a Seat at the Table for Harlem

D9 Harlem Votes® lives, loves, and serves Harlem. For us, that means centering Harlem, being present in the community, and remaining in conversation with our neighbors every day.

Our civic engagement does not end at the voter registration table. Voter registration activates our ongoing, proactive participation in local government and in the decisions that shape our community. Voting is an action, but civic engagement is a practice. We stay informed, stay engaged, ask questions, follow the policies, and pay attention to what happens long after the polls close.
