ABOUT US

We Serve Harlem First.

Established in 2024, D9 Harlem Votes® is a nonpartisan civic education coalition comprised of members of historically Black fraternities and sororities serving Harlem, New York.

D9 Harlem Votes® is an individual-based civic coalition, not a chapter-based council. Our shared focus is Harlem: educating, engaging, and registering voters while strengthening civic participation across the community.

Steering Committee Co-ChairDrew K. Gabriel

HOW IT STARTED

A Harlem call to civic duty and social action.

D9 Harlem Votes® grew from a call convened by Rev. Michael A. Walrond, Jr., Senior Pastor of First Corinthian Baptist Church (FCBC) in Harlem, New York and a member of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc., and Kercena A. Dozier, Esq., Chair of FCBC's Social Justice Ministry.

Harlem residents and members of Harlem-based historically Black Greek-letter organizations were called together to renew a commitment to civic duty and social action — to educate, engage, and register Harlem voters.
