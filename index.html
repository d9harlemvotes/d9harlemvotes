We Serve Harlem First.

Established in 2024, D9 Harlem Votes® is a nonpartisan civic education coalition led by Steering Committee Members from historically Black fraternities and sororities serving Harlem, New York.

D9 Harlem Votes® is an individual-based civic coalition, not a chapter-based council. Our shared focus is Harlem: educating, engaging, and registering voters while strengthening civic participation across the community.

HOW IT STARTED

A Harlem call to civic duty and social action.

D9 Harlem Votes® grew from a call convened by Rev. Michael A. Walrond, Jr., Senior Pastor of First Corinthian Baptist Church (FCBC) in Harlem, New York and a member of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc., and Kercena A. Dozier, Esq., Chair of FCBC's Social Justice Ministry.

Harlem residents and members of Harlem-based historically Black Greek-letter organizations were called together to renew a commitment to civic duty and social action — to educate, engage, and register Harlem voters.

A Civic Engagement Coalition Serving Harlem

D9 Harlem Votes® is led by a Steering Committee of leaders devoted to Harlem and committed to service.

We come to this work through residency, organizational service, social action, and a shared responsibility to the Harlem community. Our devotion to Harlem is rooted in its rich cultural and political legacy, and in our honor for the pioneers of civil rights and voting rights activism who paved the way for us to serve, organize, and lead.

We Serve Harlem First.

Our coalition exists because Harlem’s voice matters. We organize, educate, and engage so that our community remains informed, connected, and ready to activate its voice through the power of the vote in every election and every generation.
