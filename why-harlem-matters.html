Reference Note

The ballot figures above are official Total Applicable Ballots from NYC Board of Elections certification reports for contested Democratic Assembly primaries held June 23, 2026. They should not be described as “Black voter turnout”; official NYC election results do not identify individual voters by race.

There was no comparable Republican Assembly primary across these four New York County Assembly districts in the NYC Board of Elections’ published June 23, 2026 results, so the page does not present a direct Democratic-versus-Republican Harlem comparison.

Primary sources: NYC Board of Elections — Primary Election, June 23, 2026; NYC Community Boards — Manhattan Boards.
